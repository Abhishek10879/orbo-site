Dearsy Support

Remember birthdays, anniversaries, festivals, and everyday routines.

Find help in Dearsy

  • Add dates: Tap + on Home. Add a date manually, or choose the people and dates to import in Settings.
  • Reminders: Open a date to edit reminders. If an alert is missing, check Dearsy’s notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Widgets: Touch and hold your iPhone Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Dearsy.
  • Purchases: Go to Settings → Dearsy Pro → Restore purchases. Manage yearly renewals in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Free dates: Keep up to 10 date cards active. You can change which cards are active in Dearsy.

Contact us

Email Dearsy support. Please include your app and iOS versions and the steps to reproduce the issue. Keep personal or health information out of your email.
