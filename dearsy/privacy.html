Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Dearsy does not run servers that store your data. We do not collect, sell, or track your personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Names, dates, optional phone numbers, selected photos, notes, gift ideas, wishes, medicine schedules and dose history, and your settings are stored on your device.

Optional iCloud features

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your private iCloud account. If you create or join a Family Circle, the selected dates and medicines and their dose records are shared through iCloud with the members you invite. You can stop sharing at any time. Dearsy’s developer cannot access your private iCloud data.

Cycle tracking and Apple Health

Period and cycle data stays on your device and is excluded from iCloud sync, Family Circle sharing, and app backups. If you grant Health permission, Dearsy can read and save menstrual flow records in Apple Health. You control these permissions in iOS.

Permissions

  • Contacts and Calendar are used to import the people and dates you choose.
  • Notifications and Alarms are used for reminders and medicine alarms.
  • Photos permission lets you include or save images.
  • Face ID can lock your cycle tracker if you choose.

You can change these permissions in Settings.

Sharing and purchases

If you choose to send a wish in Messages or WhatsApp, iOS opens your chosen app and its privacy policy applies. Apple processes purchases. We do not receive your payment details. Dearsy has no ads, analytics, tracking, sign-in, or third-party SDKs.

Health information

Medication reminders and cycle estimates are for personal tracking. They are not medical advice, and cycle estimates are not a method of contraception.

Deleting data

Delete individual items in Dearsy, delete cycle records in Settings → Cycle tracking, or delete the app to remove its local data. You can manage synced data in your Apple Account’s iCloud settings.

Children and policy changes

Dearsy does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here.
