Expense & Split Tracker for iPhone

Need help? Email appdocklabs01@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. Orbo has no account and no server, so we can't see or recover it. Use Settings → Back up everything regularly and keep the file in Files or iCloud Drive.

Moving to a new iPhone

On the old iPhone: Settings → Back up everything. On the new one: install Orbo, then Settings → Restore from backup and pick that file.

Subscription

Logging, the monthly view and splits are free forever. New users get Orbo Pro free for up to 3 months. After that, Orbo Pro is ₹99 a month or ₹999 a year, with launch prices of ₹59 a month for 6 months or ₹699 for the first year. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Changed phones? Open Orbo → Settings → Orbo Pro → Restore Purchases.

Voice logging doesn't work

Voice logging needs on-device speech recognition for your language. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed for Orbo.

Circles won't sync

Both iPhones need to be on the same Wi-Fi with Orbo open, and Local Network allowed for Orbo in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Privacy Policy

Orbo on Android

Orbo is also on Google Play. Circles and Siri/Shortcuts are iPhone-only; everything else works the same. Backups move both ways: on one phone, Settings → Back up everything; on the other, Settings → Restore from backup.

Voice logging on Android

If your phone can't recognise speech by itself, Orbo asks once whether to use your phone's voice typing (usually Google). Allow the microphone for Orbo in Android Settings → Apps → Orbo → Permissions. For offline voice typing, download your language in Android Settings → Google → Voice → Offline speech recognition. You can switch this off in Orbo → Settings → Voice entry.
